I am Junteng Liu (刘俊腾), a first-year PhD candidate at the [HKUST NLP Group](https://hkust-nlp.github.io/), [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://hkust.edu.cn/), advised by [Prof. Junxian He](https://jxhe.github.io/). I received my B.Eng. from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn/) in June 2024, where I was also previously advised by Prof. Junxian He during my undergraduate studies.

My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP)** and **machine learning (ML)**. My research interests include:
- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**
